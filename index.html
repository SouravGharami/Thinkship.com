FAQ

Questions We Always Get

You're a new company — can I trust you?+
Absolutely. Think Ship is newly launched as a company, but our team brings serious depth — years of industry experience building real products for real businesses. We also offer milestone-based billing so you only pay as work is delivered. Our work speaks for itself.
How long does a typical project take?+
A standard 5-page website takes 2–3 weeks. A full web app or e-commerce platform is typically 6–12 weeks. We give you a precise timeline during the free discovery call — before you commit to anything.
What if I’m not happy with the design?+
We offer unlimited revisions during the design phase until you’re 100% satisfied — before a single line of code is written. If after 3 full concepts you’re still not happy, we refund your deposit. No questions asked.
Do you provide hosting and maintenance?+
Yes. We host on AWS, Azure, or GCP and manage it on a monthly retainer — server monitoring, backups, SSL, security patches, and priority support. From ₹5,000/month depending on stack complexity.
Is my idea protected? Do you sign NDAs?+
Absolutely. We sign NDAs before any discovery call if requested. Our standard contract includes full IP transfer on final payment. Your idea, your code, your assets — always.
What is your payment structure?+
Typically 40% deposit / 40% at design sign-off / 20% on launch. For longer projects we offer monthly milestone billing. We accept bank transfer, UPI, Razorpay, PayPal, and Wise for international clients.
